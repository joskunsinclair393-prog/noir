Access

A verified email and owner approval are required. The initial tester limit is five, excluding the owner. Only the owner can expand it.

Chat and Projects

Sent chat text, usage information, preferences and saved chat Projects are stored through NOIR’s existing backend and Supabase. Account data remains private. Previously created workspace files are retained privately for potential future development; editing and sharing those files are currently unavailable.

AI and voice processing

AI requests send necessary conversation and authorized file context to the configured provider. Free models have availability and quota limits. Think requires a compatible free reasoning model. Browser speech recognition and some playback voices may use external processing. NOIR does not record or store raw audio. Voice is turn-based, not a live audio conversation.

Usage and feedback

Normal quotas and rate limits apply to Chat and Think. Analytics use aggregated request counts and token totals, not full conversations. Feedback is sent only when you choose to submit it and is linked to your account for abuse prevention. Do not include credentials or sensitive information.

Beta limitations

AI answers can be wrong. Review generated content before relying on it. Browser microphone behavior and live-provider availability can vary. Review important answers before relying on them.

Privacy and terms drafts are under owner review before a wider launch. Use the in-app Feedback control for questions or bug reports.

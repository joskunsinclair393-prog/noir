04 / Smart Route

Start with the question.

When configured, Smart Route selects from eligible models based on the task and falls back when a supported model is unavailable.

05 / Verify

Give an answer a second look.

Verify checks a saved answer with an independent configured model and highlights possible problems or missing evidence. Available with Plus when premium providers are configured.

06 / Projects

Keep the work together.

Group related conversations and add project instructions. Open a saved chat later and continue it with the context you already built.

07 / Challenge

One prompt. Different approaches.

Compare blind answers from supported models, then see how their approaches differ. Challenge requires Plus, configured premium providers, and available spending limits.

08 / Built around you

Make it work your way.

Choose your preferred tone, language, and answer length. Search your saved conversations when you want to pick up an earlier idea.

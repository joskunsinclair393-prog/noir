# NOIR: staged beta launch plan

Keep the current five-tester limit. Do not open public signup access or enable billing. The owner alone may expand to 25 and then 100 through Command Center → Beta Applications.

## Stage 1 — five trusted testers

Personally send five approved testers the Tally form / access instructions. Approve their verified emails manually. Ask them to complete one real task, then return on a second day. Prioritize sign-in, saved chats, a Studio document/diagram, one export and one voice interaction. Record problems using in-app Feedback; never request passwords or full private conversations. Test collaborative viewer/editor access with two approved accounts.

## Stage 2 — twenty-five

Expand only after critical issues are fixed, real free-cloud responses and Office/microphone checks pass, and privacy/terms are reviewed. Check free-provider quotas, Supabase storage, error rates, server memory and support workload before changing the owner-controlled limit. Daily activity/retention metrics must come from existing real aggregated records; empty metrics are not evidence of retention.

## Stage 3 — one hundred

Expand only after a week of stable use at 25, verified backups/recovery, affordable capacity, access-control regression tests, and an incident/support plan. Do not assume free-provider quotas can serve 100 active people. Keep expansion reversible by revoking access; do not delete accounts or data.

## Organic outreach — manual, targeted

- Reddit: read each relevant AI/productivity/student community's current rules and self-promotion policy. Share a genuine build story and specific use case only where permitted. No mass posting or unsolicited DMs.
- Project showcase websites: submit an accurate description with a beta application link, supported features and known limits. Never present a concept as operational.
- Short videos: record real Chat → Studio editing, a real export, and a permission-based Voice request. Remove emails, tokens, conversations and private files. Label illustrative data.
- AI/technology communities: ask for feedback on a concrete workflow; follow moderator instructions and share results rather than repetitive advertisements.
- Social platforms: publish occasional honest progress updates and practical examples. Track returning testers and useful feedback, not just registrations.

No promotional content has been published or messages sent. No paid campaign or subscription is authorized by this plan.

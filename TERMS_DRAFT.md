# NOIR beta terms draft — owner and legal review required

This draft does not activate a new binding agreement. Complete operator/contact details, applicable law, liability terms and consumer-law review before publication or broader access.

NOIR is an invitation-only experimental workspace. Access requires an authenticated, verified, approved email; the owner may revoke beta access without deleting the underlying account. Do not share credentials, abuse integrations, bypass usage controls, upload unlawful content or attempt unauthorized access.

AI output can be inaccurate. Review answers, documents, diagrams and executable prototype code. Do not rely on NOIR as a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial or other professional judgment. Do not submit credentials or highly sensitive information to AI, feedback or generated prototypes.

Provider quotas, outages, rate limits and storage limits apply. Think requires a supported free reasoning model. No guaranteed availability, unlimited third-party usage or paid model access is promised. No payment collection or pricing change is introduced by this update.

Users remain responsible for their input, output, project-sharing decisions and permission to use third-party content. Shared members may retain content they have already viewed or downloaded. Website prototypes are frontend-only and are not deployed or hosted automatically.

Support, ownership/IP terms, suspension/appeal, data export/deletion and discontinuation procedures need owner review before a public launch.
